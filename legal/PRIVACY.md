# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 13, 2026

At Astralyr, accessible from https://astralyr.app, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how it is used, and how your data remains under your control.

---

### 1. Offline-First & Local Storage
Astralyr is built from the ground up as a private, local-first application:
- Your local music files, MP3 metadata, and playlists are stored exclusively on your own computer (`userData/library/` and IndexedDB).
- We do not upload your personal music files, listening history, or audio library to any remote servers.

### 2. Licensing & Device Verification
When you activate an Astralyr Desktop Pro license key:
- Your license key and an anonymized device identifier (e.g. computer name and OS) are transmitted directly to our public payment provider (LemonSqueezy or DodoPayments) to verify activation limits (up to 2 devices).
- Validated activations are cached on your computer with a 30-day offline grace period.
- No personal audio files or listening statistics are transmitted during license validation.

### 3. Third-Party Services
- **LRCLIB / Synced Lyrics:** When searching for synchronized lyrics, track title and artist name are queried against public open-source lyrics databases. **Lyrics may come from third-party sources.**
- **Audius:** When streaming music through the Audius integration, requests are directed to public Audius discovery nodes. **Audius tracks are subject to each artist's license.**

### 4. No Advertising & No Telemetry Tracking
Astralyr contains no third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking scripts, or data brokers.
